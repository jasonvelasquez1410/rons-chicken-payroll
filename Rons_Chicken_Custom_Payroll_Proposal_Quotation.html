1. Project Objective & Scope of Deliverables

To replace tedious manual Excel computations and error-prone payroll processing with a fast, tailored, offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) customized specifically for Ron's Chicken Cugman branch.

⚡ 1. Deli e3960 USB Attendance Parser

1-Click ingestion of the raw Employee Attendance Record.xls export. Parses punch intervals, handles overnight roasting shifts, late/undertime, and meal breaks for all 31 staff in under 1 second.

⚖️ 2. DOLE & BIR Statutory Payroll Engine

Automated computation of Daily/Hourly rates, Regular Overtime (125%), Night Shift Differential (10%), Food Allowance (₱50/day), SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR Tax exemptions.

📅 3. Interactive Cutoff Date Controller

Pre-configured standard cutoffs (1st–15th & 16th–end of month) plus custom date pickers with instant recalculation of hours, pay, and payslips.

💳 4. Cash Advance (*Vale*) & Loan Ledger

Full tracking of employee advances and emergency loans with customizable installment splits per cutoff and automatic itemization on payslips.

🖨️ 5. 1-Click Batch Printable Payslips

Standardized, official single-line header payslips ready for bulk printing or thermal/letter paper signatures upon salary disbursement.

🛡️ 6. Continuous Auto-Backup & $0 Server Fees

Automated local snapshots, persistent browser storage protection, and 1-click full .json database export/import with lifetime ownership.
